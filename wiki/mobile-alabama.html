Mobile, Alabama

A celebrated destination, especially for one particular Moe’s.

On this page

  1. In the lore
  2. Getting there
  3. Gallery

In the lore

In MMORP lore, Mobile is celebrated as a place even better than heaven, with particular praise reserved for its Moe’s. Both Slim and John Harper are said to enjoy eating there.

Getting there

Signs near the Derelict Camp point toward Mobile, but the notes warn that following the signs will not work for everyone. They are intended for Slim.
