Slim

The eccentric, dangerous inhabitant of the Derelict Camp.

On this page

  1. About
  2. In the stories

About

Slim is the main inhabitant of the Derelict Camp and is described as eccentric, dangerous, and inclined to consume everything in his path. He effectively runs the camp and sometimes wears shades.

In the stories

In the German story, Slim interrupts MMORP and Orange Orange’s visit to the camp. The two escape in MMORP’s MiG-25.

The MMORP 3D notes also include an in-universe story in which a version of John Harper Tarleton is trapped at the camp and consumed by Slim.
