Overview

Joeltech is a company in the MMORP universe. Its projects range from the MMORP games to a phone and the Gale supercar, with headquarters on the Joeltech Archipelago.

Products

The Gale is a high-performance supercar with a Sprat trim. The Joeltech Phone pairs an extraordinary price with a JTX 6090 mobile chip. MMORP is also described in the archive as a Joeltech success.
