Gulper

A slow-moving MMORP 3D enemy with two distinct variants.

On this page

  1. Behavior
  2. Variants

Behavior

Gulpers move slowly toward the player and spawn from a spawner ball. They are exclusive to MMORP 3D.

Variants

The tongued Gulper fires a smelly projectile. The toothed Gulper does not, so the two variants create different threats despite their similar movement.
