Helper

A rare moment when running into an enemy is a good idea.

On this page

  1. Behavior
  2. Connections
  3. Gallery

Behavior

Helpers spawn at random positions and orientations. Unlike most enemies, they deal negative damage: touching one restores health.

Their level works as a mid-game breather, giving players a chance to stock up before the more dangerous Chompers. Helpers do not appear in MMORP 3D’s infinite mode.

Connections

Helpers are related to Threnemies. According to an in-universe rumor, Prickly projectiles grow up to become Helpers because of their similar appearance.
