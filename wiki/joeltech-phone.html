Joeltech Phone

An outrageously expensive phone with an equally outrageous chip.

On this page

Overview

The Joeltech Phone is a Joeltech product listed at $9,999.99. The notes say its high price was unpopular with many users.

Hardware

It includes a JTX 6090 mobile chip, described in the project’s tongue-in-cheek lore as making it the fastest phone ever made.
