The clan

Darna is the base of the Darna clan, described in the MMORP lore as a powerful force trying to take over and end the Derelict Camp.

Tactics

The clan’s notes mention wall breakers and a “barb cycle” using evolved barbarians, including the top-secret evolved barb hut. The archive also preserves a Great Darna II clan description; its roster and statistics are a snapshot rather than necessarily current.
