Behavior

Squelches are the first enemies in the original MMORP and in MMORP 3D’s classic mode. They emerge from a ball and travel in straight paths.

In MMORP 3D they can also hatch from Squelch eggs. Several enemy sources can produce them, making Squelches one of the most common threats in the game.

Strategy

Keep an eye on where a Squelch starts moving: its straight path makes its direction useful information when finding a gap.
