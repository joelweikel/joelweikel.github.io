About

MMORP is the main character of the MMORP series and the player’s character throughout its games. His shape has been compared to a heart, although his design has a character all its own.

His appearance has changed with the series: the colorful Scratch original became a fully modeled character in MMORP 3D.

In the games

In the original game, MMORP dodges ten levels of enemies using the keyboard. MMORP 3 moved the character toward the mouse, a control scheme continued in MMORP 3D.

Beyond the games

MMORP appears in other projects, including Starschlop. In the German story, he meets Orange Orange at the Derelict Camp and offers him a place with better Wi-Fi.
