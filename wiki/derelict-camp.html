Derelict Camp

A not entirely welcoming camp at the heart of the lore.

On this page

  1. Overview
  2. Stories and surroundings
  3. Gallery
  4. Video

Overview

The Derelict Camp is a recurring location associated with Slim and Edina. Slim is its main inhabitant, while Edina’s house is also found at the camp.

Stories and surroundings

In the German story, Orange Orange arrives at the camp, meets MMORP, and eventually flees with him when Slim appears.

Signs at the camp point toward Mobile, Alabama, although the lore warns that following them will not get an ordinary traveler there. The Darna clan is described as a force seeking to take over and end the camp.
