Overview

The Gale is a Joeltech supercar powered by a twincharged Joeltech V8. The base model is rated in the project notes at 1,000 horsepower and a 0–60 mph time of 3.0 seconds.

Gale Sprat

The Gale Sprat raises the stated output to 1,670 horsepower and the 0–60 mph time to 2.1 seconds. It also has a 170-horsepower electric motor driving the front wheels.
