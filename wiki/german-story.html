The German Story

MMORP, Orange Orange, a dangerous camp, and the search for Wi-Fi.

On this page

  1. Plot
  2. Escape
  3. Original

Plot

Orange Orange wanders into the Derelict Camp while playing Clash Royale. He meets MMORP, who offers him access to a better Wi-Fi connection at his house.

Escape

A boom and a fire announce Slim’s arrival. MMORP and Orange Orange escape in MMORP’s MiG-25 and fly away in search of good Wi-Fi.

Original

The story was written in German. The original document is available below, and the summary here follows its events rather than translating every line.
