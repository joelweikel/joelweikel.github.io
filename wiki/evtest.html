Evtest

The original game’s passcode-powered developer menu.

On this page

  1. Overview
  2. Known codes
  3. Visual effects

Overview

Evtest is a recurring developer feature that asks for a passcode and enables different tools or cheats. Its name is a play on “devtest.”

Known codes

In the original game, yes answers the button’s “Evtest?” prompt and enables a mode that adds one health per tick. Even that regeneration may not save the player from Tennemy.

The original Scratch notes also list billy for 100 lives and EZ for easy mode. These can help players practice enemy patterns.

Visual effects

Some experimental visual settings from the first game later appeared in MMORP 3, including pixelation, warp, and color changes.
